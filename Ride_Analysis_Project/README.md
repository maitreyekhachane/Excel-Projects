## Project Overview

This project analyzes ride-booking data to understand service performance, ride trends, customer payment preferences, and revenue contribution across different ride categories.

Using SQL and Excel, the project explores ride completion, cancellations, fare patterns, service-wise revenue, and business performance to identify opportunities for improving operations and revenue.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze total rides and ride completion status.
- Compare demand across different ride services.
- Evaluate revenue contribution by service type.
- Analyze average ride distance and fare.
- Identify customer payment preferences.
- Understand ride cancellations and operational patterns.

## Dataset Information

- Total Ride Records: **50,000**
- Completed Rides: **44,964**
- Cancelled Rides: **5,036**
- Total Fare Revenue: **₹2.46 Crore**
- Average Ride Distance: **25.53 km**
- Average Fare: **₹547.39**
- Data Period: **June–August 2024**

## Key Insights

- Bike services contributed the highest revenue, accounting for approximately **30.2%** of total fare revenue.
- Auto services contributed approximately **24.8%** of total revenue.
- Out of 50,000 rides, 44,964 were completed.
- The overall average ride distance was approximately 25.53 km.
- Paytm was the most frequently recorded payment method.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on improving the availability of high-revenue services such as Bike and Auto.
- Investigate cancellation patterns to identify possible operational issues.
- Monitor service-wise revenue to improve resource allocation.
- Use payment preferences to support a convenient payment experience.
- Track monthly ride trends to support demand planning.
