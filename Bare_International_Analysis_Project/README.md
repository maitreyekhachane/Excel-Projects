## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail store evaluation data to assess store performance, customer experience, and the quality of service provided by style advisors.

The analysis focuses on different aspects of the in-store experience, including store ambience, first impressions, customer discovery, product recommendations, trial experience, and service closure.

Using Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, and data visualization, the project identifies performance gaps and opportunities to improve the overall retail customer experience.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall retail store performance.
- Analyze store evaluation scores and performance grades.
- Compare performance across different geographical zones.
- Identify strong and weak areas of the customer experience.
- Evaluate style advisor recommendations and customer interaction.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve service quality.

## Key Insights

- Store ambience received the highest average score of **90.33**, indicating strong performance in store presentation and environment.
- First impressions scored **80.27**, showing a generally positive initial customer experience.
- Recommendations scored **60.08**, highlighting an opportunity to improve product suggestions.
- Closure and care received the lowest score of **55.16**, identifying an important service improvement area.
- The North zone recorded the highest average evaluation score of **76.74**, while the South zone recorded the lowest at **66.61**.

## Business Recommendations

- Improve the customer interaction and product discovery process.
- Provide additional training to style advisors on product recommendations.
- Strengthen the purchase closure and customer follow-up experience.
- Share best practices from higher-performing zones with other locations.
- Conduct regular evaluations to monitor service quality and improvement.
